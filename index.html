Accounts payable

AI Invoice Reconciliation Assistant

Matching supplier invoices to the bank statement is slow manual work. This app reads PDF invoices with AI, matches each one to a bank payment, and flags what needs a person's attention.

  • Google Gemini extracts vendor, invoice number, date, tax and total from invoices in any layout.
  • Rule-based matching on amount, date window and fuzzy vendor name, so every match can be explained to an auditor.
  • Flags duplicates, amount mismatches (including swapped digits), unpaid invoices and unexplained payments.
  • Downloads a colour-coded Excel report with one sheet per exception type.
  • 50 test invoices in 3 layouts
  • 12 / 12 planted errors flagged by the matching rules
  • 84% of fields read by the no-AI baseline

Credit risk

Credit Risk Scoring Model

Predicts the probability that a loan applicant defaults, explains which factors drive the risk, and recommends Approve, Refer or Decline. Trained on 1,000 real past applicants (German Credit dataset).

  • Logistic regression compared with a decision tree, evaluated on 200 applicants the model never saw.
  • Approval cut-off chosen to minimise expected cost, since a bad loan costs about 5× more than a lost good customer.
  • Per-applicant explanation of what pushes the risk up or down.
  • AI-drafted credit memo with a number check. Sex, marital status and nationality are excluded from the model.
  • 0.805 test AUC (Gini 0.61)
  • 87% of defaulters caught
  • ~60% lower expected cost than approving everyone
